LAUNCH PROJECT

Strengthening brand awareness, one project at a time.

Capturing the attention of your target audience is the art of curating a story they'll never forget. From design and messaging to presentation and delivery, we ensure that every element of your mailer is crafted to resonate with your audience and tell your story in a compelling way.

Our Approach

With every project comes a fresh perspective. We bring personalized ideas and innovative solutions to ensure your project is an unforgettable experience.

01

Conceptualization is where our journey begins.

We understand that a successful project begins with a strong foundation. That's why conceptualization is a crucial part of our process - it's where our journey together begins.

Our team of seasoned experiential experts work in close contact with you to understand your brand, your goals, and your audience. We develop a unique creative strategy that sets the stage for your next project.

02

Sustainably-sourcing only the finest of products.

Want to make your audience laugh, gasp, cry tears of joy, or snap a selfie and post it? Put it in our hands. Our mailers are designed in- house, and include carefully selected products that are in direct correlation to your brand and your goals.

03

Mailer content is approved and production begins.

Your work is done! Sit back and relax while we handle the sourcing, customization and fabrication of your mailer.

All of our vendors are industry trusted veterans with whom we have worked for years; your projects are always in excellent hands.

04

Fulfilling an unmatched mailer.

Every mailer is packaged in-house by the Kurate Team, giving each box a white glove touch.

Now it’s time to ship! Whether your recipients are domestic or international, we run a tight ship when coordinating deliveries for timely arrivals, no matter what.

Our Services

From ideation to execution, we keep your brand identity and objectives at the center of our workflow.

Entertainment Mailers

Entertainment mailers are a versatile way to promote films and shows within the Entertainment Industry. Whether it's content-specific packaging and promo items for a fun, at-home viewing kit or creating an immersive, themed experience, we do it all.

PR Packages

Seeding and Activation kits are a must in today's Influencer driven world. Allow us to use our expertise to create stunning packaging that not only elevates your product, but produces a buzzworthy campaign that captures the attention of your target audience..

Corporate Gifting

Our corporate gifting services cater to a wide range of needs, including, but not limited to, Holiday gifts for clients, vendors, and staff, specialized gifts within the entertainment industry for talent, client experiences, as well as group gifting options that extend beyond traditional industry mailers and influencer boxes.

PR/influencer Lists

We have access to PR and Influencer lists that are meticulously curated, ensuring a comprehensive network of industry professionals and influential personalities to effectively amplify brand messaging and maximize exposure

Product Packaging

Is your retail packaging in need of a revamp? Let our team combine creativity and functionality to create visually stunning designs that fully reflect your brand's unique identity.

Bespoke Packaging

In addition to corrugated mailers, clamshells and telescope boxes, there also lies the Bespoke Box. One of a kind with custom colors, shapes and endless additional features to match your brand and style: metal and acrylic finishes, 3D effects, full artwork print, fabrics, textures, lighting, audio visual effects, and even motion! At Kurate your vision becomes a reality.
